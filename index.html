Optimal versus naive diversification

A replication of DeMiguel, Garlappi and Uppal (2009) on Ken French's data, and an extension in which a portfolio of French's 49 industries tracks their combination weighted by market value under the constraints an enhanced index fund runs: no short positions, bounded deviations from the index, limited trading each month, three carbon-intensive industries held at half their index weight, tobacco and weapons excluded, and a beta (the slope of the portfolio's return on the index's) of one to the index. The code, the thirteen notebooks, the tables and the README with the findings are in the repository; the dashboard and the companion page are built from the tables committed with them, and the note cites them.

The dashboardThe results of the thirteen notebooks in ten interactive figures, each naming the tables it is drawn from; the numbers appear on hover. The companion pageThe attribution, the split of the portfolio's return relative to the index into six parts, one per factor (a factor is a return series that many assets move with, such as size or value), and a remainder, worked through on one page: the map of the industries' betas to the factors, the arithmetic from a change in weight to its effect on return, a calculator, and three cases. The noteThe write-up as a PDF, with its HTML source beside it in the repository: the replication beside the published figures, the extended sample, the estimators under the constraints, the attribution, and the limits of the design. The repository on GitHubCode, notebooks, tables and the README; the MIT licence; how to run everything from a blank Colab runtime.
